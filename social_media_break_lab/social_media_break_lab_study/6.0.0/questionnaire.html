You are invited to take part in a research study conducted by the University of Leeds. Before deciding whether to participate, please read the information below carefully. Why your answers matter

Information · why_accurate · ybm_intro_consent.why_it_matters

There are no right or wrong answers here

What you tell us goes into research on how sleep, screens and feelings fit together for people your age, and on what actually helps. That only works if the answers are really yours, including the ones that aren't flattering.

A rushed or made-up answer doesn't just lose one number. It makes the whole picture slightly less true — for everyone the research is meant to help.

So take your time. And if you'd rather not answer something, leave it blank — that's always allowed.
