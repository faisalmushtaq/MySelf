MyPhone/MyBrain Intervention Study

Information · study_intro · ybm_intro_consent.intro

Welcome back!

You are now completing the follow-up questionnaire for the My Phone My Brain study after your social media break.

We will ask you some questions about your wellbeing and digital behaviour so that we can compare your responses before and after the break. Your information will continue to be handled confidentially and stored securely in accordance with University of Leeds data-protection procedures.

Taking part remains voluntary. You can stop at any time or ask a member of the research team if anything is unclear.

This questionnaire should take approximately 10 minutes to complete. Please answer the questions carefully and as accurately as you can.

Why your answers matter

Information · why_accurate · ybm_intro_consent.why_it_matters

There are no right or wrong answers here

What you tell us goes into research on how sleep, screens and feelings fit together for people your age, and on what actually helps. That only works if the answers are really yours, including the ones that aren't flattering.

A rushed or made-up answer doesn't just lose one number. It makes the whole picture slightly less true — for everyone the research is meant to help.

So take your time. And if you'd rather not answer something, leave it blank — that's always allowed.
